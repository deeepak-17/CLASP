## 6. Reproducibility

Both partitions are pure functions of `(corpus, seed, strategy, strategy config)` — `partitions/strategies.py`'s strategies do no I/O and no non-deterministic operations (the per-developer hash-chunk fallback uses `sha256(seed:cluster_id:file_id)`, not a random-number generator). Re-running `scripts/build_partitions.py` with either config against the same `datasets/processed/corpus.jsonl` reproduces byte-identical shard digests — exercised directly by `tests/test_partitions_per_developer.py`'s determinism tests.
